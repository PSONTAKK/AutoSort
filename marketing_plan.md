# AutoSort Marketing & Monetization Strategy

## 1. How to Make Money (Monetization Strategy)
Utility apps usually fail if they charge upfront because people want to know if it works first. The best approach is the Freemium Model.

* **Freemium Model (Recommended)**: Give away the core app for free (e.g., max 3 active rules, local sorting only). 
* **AutoSort PRO**: Charge a $4.99 One-Time Lifetime Unlock for unlimited rules, cloud sync, and advanced features. People hate subscriptions for small utility apps and love one-time purchases.
* **Ad-Supported (Alternative)**: If you want to keep it 100% free, you can add a small banner ad at the bottom of the Dashboard, or require them to watch a short video ad to unlock a new rule slot. However, for an app that mostly runs in the background, In-App Purchases (Pro version) make much more money than ads.

## 2. Who is your Target Audience?
* **Students/Researchers**: Downloading PDFs, research papers, and assignments that end up cluttering their downloads folder.
* **Freelancers/Accountants**: Downloading invoices, receipts, and tax documents that need to be backed up to Google Drive automatically.
* **Memers/Designers**: People who download hundreds of images and want them auto-sorted into specific meme or asset folders.

## 3. How and Where to Promote (Marketing)

### A. Short-Form Video (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels)
*This is currently the #1 way to get app downloads for free.*
* **The Hook**: Show a messy, chaotic Android Downloads folder.
* **The Solution**: Say "Tired of this? I built an app that fixes it automatically."
* **The Demo**: Record your phone screen. Download a file called "Invoice.pdf", and show a notification popping up instantly saying it was moved to the Google Drive "Taxes" folder.
* **Hashtags**: `#androidtips`, `#productivity`, `#techtok`, `#appdeveloper`.

### B. Reddit (Highly targeted)
*Do not just post ads; Redditors hate that. Post your app as a "Showoff" or asking for feedback.*
* **r/AndroidApps**: Post on "Saturday App Appreciation" or ask for beta testers.
* **r/Productivity**: Explain the workflow: "I was tired of manually organizing my downloads, so I built an app that reads file names and auto-sorts them to Google Drive."
* **r/DataHoarder & r/Organization**: People here love automating file management.

### C. Product Hunt & Hacker News
When you officially launch the app on the Play Store, make a launch post on ProductHunt.com. It's a website where tech enthusiasts look for cool new tools. A good launch here can get you your first 500-1,000 users in a single day.

### D. ASO (App Store Optimization)
When you write your Google Play Store description, use the exact words people search for.
* **Don't just call it**: "AutoSort"
* **Call it**: "AutoSort: File Manager & Auto Folder Organizer"
* **Keywords to include in your description**: Auto move files, organize downloads, sync to Google Drive, automatic file sorter, background file manager.
